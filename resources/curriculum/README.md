# 2026–2027 Physics Annual Teaching Plan

This package provides the supplied school plan for the website. It does not revise the school curriculum, dates, teaching objectives, assessment schedule or equations.

- `2026-2027_Physics_Annual_Teaching_Plan.docx` is the unchanged original, with a stable download filename.
- `2026-2027_Physics_Annual_Teaching_Plan.pdf` is a **layout adapted PDF reading copy**, 15 A4 landscape pages.
- `2026-2027_Physics_Annual_Teaching_Plan_Preview.png` previews its first page.
- `source.json` records filenames, hashes and verification details.

## PDF reading copy

Rendered with the Codex bundled LibreOffice and the documents skill `render_docx.py --emit_pdf`. A temporary fontconfig makes the existing Traditional Chinese Kai font available, preserving the Chinese assessment note. Rendering uses the original source filename so its filename field is retained. The PDF metadata title identifies it as a reading copy; the original DOCX metadata is untouched.

Two long word-equation lines overflowed their table cells in the unadapted export. In a private temporary clone only, those two lines were set as 8 pt italic text using the exact original words and symbols. No equation content was corrected or rewritten. These fit adjustments affect pages 12 and 13 only. The temporary clone is not a website download.

## Verification and limits

- 15 PDF pages and 15 rendered page images produced.
- Every page was visually inspected; Chinese text, tables and equations were checked.
- After the fit adjustment, pages 1–11 and 14–15 were pixel-identical to the inspected unadapted export. Pages 12–13 were inspected again and both long equations fit within the objective column.
- The ordered text content of the source and private reading-copy clone was checked to be identical.
- The original download hash was checked again after rendering.
- Word and LibreOffice may paginate or render fonts differently. The PDF is a reading copy with the two disclosed layout adjustments, not a replacement for the original DOCX. No claim is made that the original plan has been fact-checked or updated.
